**Olympus went PvE. The hunt did not.**

At launch, Asmongold put the Olympus network on the PvE ruleset: a different megaserver from the PvP one, and the PvP server is faction-locked. So an Olympus nameplate is never walking past you on this realm, and Godslayer was built to wait for one. No more.

**Every player kill counts now, one point each.** Your titles climb on the whole war, not on a guild that is on another server. The leaderboard fills. The brag button has something to brag about on day one.

- **Name a rival for three points a kill.** `/godslayer rival <guild>`, or let the addon pick the guild with the most blood between you (your deaths to them count double). The hit list, the spotter and the dossiers all follow your rival the way they used to follow Olympus. `/godslayer rival off` turns the nominating off; every kill still counts its one point.
- **Olympus is still wired, and still pays five.** If the network ever does turn up on your server, or a lone Olympian wanders in, the five-point bounty, the regicide call and the whole pantheon of brag lines come straight back. They just keep themselves in reserve until there is a real Olympus kill behind them.
- **A new set of brag lines for the faction war**, with the Olympus ones held back until you earn them.

**Your old kills are safe, and better off.** If you played 2.4 through 2.6, the kills you landed on random enemies were counted but scored at zero, which quietly kept you off your guild's leaderboard. The first time you log into 2.7 those kills are lifted to a point apiece, your points catch up to your kills, and you reappear on the board where you belonged. Guildmates still on the old version show up correctly too, low scores and all, instead of being dropped as if they were cheating.

Nothing about how it plays changed: no automation, no combat log, it only ever speaks in your own guild chat and only when you click. Regenerated against the launch-day client (build 1.60.1.70790), 6,082 checks green, and the cost profiler still passes with room to spare.

**How to update:** on CurseForge, hit Update. With Yeeterson Forge it arrives by itself. By hand: download **Godslayer-2.7.0.zip** below, close the game fully, replace the `Godslayer` folder in `_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`, start the game. Your kills, titles and leaderboard are kept.
