**Every hunter on your faction, on one board.**

**New: the Hunters board.** Leaderboard tab, press **Hunters**: everyone running Godslayer on your faction and ruleset, across realms, ranked by points, **Lifetime** or **This week**.
- Scores are self-reported, and a player only ranks once their own Godslayer has sent you their score directly. Nobody can post a score for someone else.
- It fills as your game finds hunters, so tell friends to update.
- This week ranks from the next weekly reset; until then rows show "tracking began mid-week".
- It shares your character name, class, kill counts, Olympus kills and rung by hidden whisper with Godslayer users of your faction and ruleset who ask. Their copies keep it up to 7 days.
- Rather not? `/godslayer hunters off`, or untick **Share on the Hunters board** in Options, AddOns, Godslayer. Sharing stops at once, but copies others already have cannot be erased.
- To find hunters it joins a hidden channel, GodslayerHunters. It stays out of your chat windows and nothing is posted in it.

**Flag calls, fixed.** Thanks Dirtydeeds. Calls now come from the battleground's own messages. A flag dropped and grabbed again within 2.5 seconds is not called, and drop and return calls for one flag wait 30 seconds before speaking again. By default only flag taken, first drop, captured and your flag returned speak. `/godslayer flag calls all` or `off`; `/godslayer flag voice off` mutes the voice, banners stay.

**Its own volume.** `/godslayer volume 0-100`, or the slider in Options, AddOns, Godslayer. Addons get no volume knob for their own sounds, so below 100 Godslayer sets your Dialog volume to that level while its line plays, then puts yours back. An NPC talking at that moment plays at that level too.

**Hidden kills count.** Forever sometimes hides who you killed from addons. Those kills now score when the game confirms the honorable kill, as Unknown victim.

**Fixes.** Guildmates see a late score upgrade or new rung right away. An Olympian spotted mid-fight switches to your kill.mp3. Late killing blows are credited. Past 10,000 victims old one-off records fold into your totals; Olympians, regicides and repeat victims stay. A broken copy can't flood the threat board. Announcer off also silences queued lines.

**Not proven live yet.** The Hunters board was tested on a simulated 220-player network, not the real servers, and the new flag calls haven't been heard in a real Warsong Gulch. Godslayer logs any flag message it doesn't recognise. If something looks off, say so here.

Your kills, titles and guild board are kept.
